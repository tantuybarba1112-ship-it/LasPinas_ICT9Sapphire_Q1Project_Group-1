About OBMC

Founded in 1966 by Dr. Preciosa S. Soliven, Operation Brotherhood Montessori Center (OBMC) pioneered the authentic Montessori system of education in the Philippines. OBMC helps students develop their skills, discover their interests, and become responsible, self-reliant, and confident individuals through hands-on learning.

Our Mission

To transform individuals into self-reliant, compassionate, and productive citizens through the Montessori curriculum that balances academic excellence, character formation, and practical life skills.

Our Vision

To be a premier educational institution recognized for developing holistic leaders equipped with critical thinking, moral integrity, and a deep respect for humanity and nature.
