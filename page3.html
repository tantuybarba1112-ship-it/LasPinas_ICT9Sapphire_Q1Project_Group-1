Admission Requirements

Prepare digital or physical copies of the following documents:

  • PSA Birth Certificate (Photocopy)
  • Latest Report Card (Form 138)
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • Two (2) 2x2 ID Pictures with white background
  • Learner Reference Number (LRN) for transferees

Enrollment Steps

  1. Complete and submit the online registration form.
  2. Wait for email confirmation and assessment schedule.
  3. Submit admission documents to the Las Piñas Registrar.
  4. Finalize enrollment and class section assignment.
